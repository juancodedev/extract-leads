## Google Maps Deep Dive

### Best Search Queries

```
Barbershops/Salons:
├─ "barbershop Santiago"
├─ "peluqueria Santiago" 
├─ "salon de belleza Las Condes"
├─ "barbershop Providencia"

Cafés/Food:
├─ "cafe Santiago"
├─ "coffee shop Las Condes"
├─ "cafe Ñuñoa"

Retail/Hardware:
├─ "ferreteria Santiago"
├─ "tienda online Chile"
├─ "minimarket Santiago"

Professional Services:
├─ "contador Santiago"
├─ "consultor contable Santiago"
├─ "abogado Santiago"

Services/Cleaning:
├─ "servicio limpieza Santiago"
├─ "cleaning service Las Condes"
```

### Data Extraction Checklist

- [ ] Business name (exact)
- [ ] Phone number (visible in listing)
- [ ] Website URL (if shown)
- [ ] Address (full street address)
- [ ] Google rating (stars and review count)
- [ ] Operating hours
- [ ] Link to website → Check for email via contact page

### Time per Search

- 15-20 minutes = 15-20 leads
- 100 leads = 5-7 hours manual search

### Pro Tips

1. **Zoom strategy:** Start broad (Santiago) → zoom to neighborhood → zoom to street
2. **Review reading:** Check reviews for pain point language:
   - "hard to get appointment" → Scheduling pain
   - "inventory issues" → Inventory management pain
   - "no online presence" → Digital transformation opportunity
3. **Call when unsure:** If contact info unclear, phone number in listing = verification that business is active
4. **Screenshot reviews:** Take screenshot of review comments mentioning pain points for pitch research

### Data Extraction from Business Pages

**From About Section:**
- [ ] Official business name
- [ ] Contact email
- [ ] Phone number
- [ ] Website link
- [ ] Hours of operation
- [ ] Address (if listed)
- [ ] Description/services

**From Activity:**
- [ ] Posting frequency (daily/weekly/monthly)
- [ ] Like count on recent posts
- [ ] Comment engagement
- [ ] Share count (viral indicator)

**From Comments:**
- [ ] Customer pain points mentioned
- [ ] Satisfaction signals

### Facebook-Specific Signals

- **Active page** = posts within last 30 days
- **Engaged audience** = comments + shares on posts
- **Contact urgency** = "Message Us" button visible = likely responds
- **WhatsApp link** = if visible, high response probability

### Time per Search

- 20-30 minutes = 20-30 leads (higher quality often)

### Search Methods

```
Hashtag Searches (Location + Industry):
├─ #peluqueriachile
├─ #salonbellezasantiago
├─ #barbershopsantiago
├─ #cafésantiago

└─ Browse business accounts in area

Follower Network:
├─ Find similar business in area
├─ Check their followers
├─ Follow competitor accounts
```

### Data Extraction from Instagram Business Profile

**From Bio:**
- [ ] Business name (handle)
- [ ] Contact email (clickable in bio)
- [ ] WhatsApp link (usually format: wa.me/+56...)
- [ ] Website (linktree or direct link)
- [ ] Phone number (sometimes in bio)

**From Content:**
- [ ] Posting frequency (daily/weekly/sporadic)
- [ ] Average likes per post (engagement metric)
- [ ] Comments (read for pain point signals)
- [ ] Stories (recent activity indicator)
- [ ] Product/service photos (business confirmation)

**From Contact Options:**
- [ ] Direct messaging (DM capability)
- [ ] Email button (if present)
- [ ] Call button (if present)
- [ ] WhatsApp button (if present)

### High-Value Signals

- **Verified business account** = legitimate
- **5+ posts per week** = engaged owner
- **50+ comments per post** = active community
- **DM button + WhatsApp** = open to contact
- **Link in bio with email** = conversion-focused

### Time per Search

- 15-20 minutes = 15-25 leads (often better quality)

```

### Navigation Steps

1. Go to www.paginasamarillas.cl
2. Search by category (dropdown list)
3. Filter by location (Región Metropolitana, specific region)
4. Sort by "nearest you" (if using location) or "relevance"
5. Click each result to extract:
   - Business name
   - Phone
   - Address
   - Website link
   - Email (if listed)

### Data Quality

- Yellow Pages = pre-categorized
- More standardized contact info than Google Maps
- Often includes address (helpful for verification)
- May include business hours

### Time per Search

- 30-45 minutes = 50-100 leads (higher volume per category)

├─ ccmetro.cl (Metropolitan Chamber)

├─ [Your region] chamber website
```

### Research Strategy

1. **Navigate to member directory**
2. **Search by:**
   - Business name
   - Industry category
   - Location/region

3. **Extract:**
   - Verified business name
   - Contact information
   - Address
   - Chamber registration date (= legitimacy signal)

### Quality Signal

- **Chamber member** = tax-compliant, legitimate, registered business
- **Preferred leads** = members often more receptive to B2B offers
- **Pre-qualified** = have already invested in membership

### Time per Search

- 45-60 minutes = 30-50 leads (high quality)

## Mercado Público (Government Supplier Registry)

### What It Is

- Government procurement platform in Chile
- Suppliers register to be eligible for public contracts
- Free, transparent registry

### Research Strategy

1. Go to www.mercadopublico.cl
2. Search by:
   - Business category
   - Business size (pequeña empresa = small business filter)
   - Region

3. Extract:
   - Official business name
   - Contact details
   - Business registration number (RUT)
   - Address
   - Contact person

### Quality Signal

- **Mercado Público registration** = tax-compliant, legitimate, audited
- Useful for **verification** of leads found elsewhere
- Shows **business type** (supplier category)

### Use Case

- If you find a lead on Google Maps, cross-reference on Mercado Público for verification
- Good for professional services (accountants, consultants, etc.)

### Time per Search

- 45-60 minutes = 20-40 leads (very high quality)

## Efficiency Tips

1. **Template is your friend:** Copy lead template + paste as you research
2. **Multi-tab browsing:** Open 5 businesses in separate tabs, extract info rapidly
3. **Take breaks:** 60 mins research = 5-10 min break (mental fatigue real)
4. **Record findings:** Write notes in real-time (painful to remember later)
5. **Review once weekly:** Verify leads are still active before outreach
6. **Tag high-priority:** Mark 80+ scored leads immediately for first batch outreach
7. **Keep backup:** Save spreadsheet locally + cloud (redundancy)
8. **A/B test:** Pick 10 hot leads per industry, test 2 message variants

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** "Found 20 leads but most have no email"
**Solution:** Use Hunter.io (free tier, 50/month) or manually check website contact page

**Problem:** "Can't verify if business is active"
**Solution:** Call (say you're researching vendors), or check if posted on social in last 30 days

**Problem:** "Too many leads, overwhelmed"
**Solution:** Sort by score (HOT first), pick one neighborhood, contact 10 per day

**Problem:** "WhatsApp number not responding"
**Solution:** Try email or call. Some owners check messages only on certain days.

**Problem:** "Website broken or outdated"
**Solution:** Still valid lead—website age doesn't correlate with receptiveness. Reach out anyway.
