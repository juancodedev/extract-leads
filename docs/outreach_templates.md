## Email Campaign Approach (Batch Sending)

### For HOT Leads (Score 80-100)

**Day 1:** WhatsApp message (personal + direct)
**Day 3:** Email if no response (friendly follow-up)
**Day 5:** Phone call or final WhatsApp

### For WARM Leads (Score 60-79)

**Day 1:** Email (more formal introduction)
**Day 4:** WhatsApp if no response (warmer tone)
**Day 7:** Final WhatsApp or move to nurture list

### For COOL Leads (Score 40-59)

**Day 1:** General email blast (less personalized)
**Week 2:** Segment by engagement, send industry-specific content
**Follow-up:** Monthly newsletter / content marketing

## Dos & Don'ts

### ✅ DO:
- Personalize with specific details (neighborhood, pain point from reviews)
- Keep first message short (WhatsApp) or medium (Email)
- Use their language (Spanish in Chile)
- Reference specific observed pain point
- Offer low-commitment call (15 min)
- Include your name and contact info
- Follow up 3-4x before giving up

### ❌ DON'T:
- Copy-paste generic message to 100 people (shows)
- Oversell features (focus on pain solution)
- Ask for too much info upfront
- Make it seem like a sales call (say "conversation")
- Include pricing in initial message
- Use English (unless they do first)
- Send during odd hours (send 9am-5pm, Mon-Fri)
- Give up after 1-2 attempts

## Message Length Guidelines

```
WhatsApp:
├─ Ideal: 100-300 words (2-5 short paragraphs)
├─ Max: 500 words (5 paragraphs before it gets annoying)
├─ Use emojis sparingly (1-2 max)
├─ Break into bullet points for features

Email:
├─ Ideal: 200-400 words
├─ Max: 500 words
├─ Use paragraphs (not walls of text)
├─ BIG improvement: personalization > longer copy

Phone/Video Call:
├─ Let them talk 60-70% (you listen)
├─ Ask open-ended questions
├─ Take notes on pain points
└─ Offer solution only after understanding need
```
