# Industry Filters & Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

## INCLUSION CRITERIA (Target These Industries)

### Primary Target Industries (Highest Priority)

#### **1. Service-Based Businesses**

**Barbershops & Hair Salons**
- Problem they solve: Manual appointment booking = customer frustration
- Key pain points: Scheduling, inventory (products), cash register tracking
- Typical size: 2-8 employees
- Payment method: Cash + Credit (local POS systems)
- Tech readiness: Medium-High (Instagram-heavy marketing)
- Chile prevalence: Very High (saturated market = competitive advantage)
- Search queries:
  ```
  "peluquería [city]", "barbershop [city]", "salon de belleza"
  "cortes hombre [city]", "cortes mujer [city]"
  ```

**Massage/Wellness Services**
- Problem: Appointment scheduling, client retention, payment tracking
- Key pain points: Calendar management, price tracking, client history
- Typical size: 1-5 employees
- Payment method: Cash + Card
- Tech readiness: Medium (Instagram followers signal)
- Search queries:
  ```
  "masaje [city]", "spa [city]", "terapia [city]", "wellness [city]"
  "reflexología", "quiropraxia"
  ```

**Professional Services (Accountants, Lawyers, Consultants)**
- Problem: Client reporting, document management, invoicing
- Key pain points: Billing cycles, client communication, document storage
- Typical size: 1-8 employees
- Payment method: Bank transfer + Credit
- Tech readiness: Medium (may have outdated systems)
- Search queries:
  ```
  "contador [city]", "auditor [city]", "consultor", "abogado"
  "contabilidad", "servicios contables", "asesoría legal"
  ```

**Cleaning Services**
- Problem: Scheduling, staff management, client billing
- Key pain points: Route optimization, team coordination, payment tracking
- Typical size: 2-10 employees
- Payment method: Bank transfer + Cash
- Tech readiness: Low-Medium (may resist tech adoption)
- Search queries:
  ```
  "servicios limpieza [city]", "cleaning service", "mantención", "aseo"
  "limpieza comercial", "limpieza residencial"
  ```

**Maintenance/Handyman Services**
- Problem: Job scheduling, material costs tracking
- Key pain points: Calendar conflicts, supplier invoices, client communication
- Typical size: 1-5 employees (often solo)
- Payment method: Cash + Bank transfer
- Tech readiness: Low (often phone-only)
- Search queries:
  ```

  "reparaciones", "instalaciones"
  ```

## EXCLUSION CRITERIA (Avoid These)

### By Company Size

**❌ 11+ Employees**
- Typically have IT departments
- May already use enterprise systems
- Decision cycles too slow
- Budget approval more complex
- Not in "micro-business" target

**Examples to exclude:**
- "ABC Solutions Inc. - 25 employees"
- "Large retail chains" (Carrefour, Ripley, etc.)
- "Corporate offices"

---

### By Industry (Regulated/Complex)

**❌ Financial Services**
- Banks, insurance, lending
- Heavy regulatory compliance
- Proprietary systems mandatory
- Not suitable for SME solution

**❌ Healthcare (Medical)**
- Hospitals, clinics with multiple doctors
- Heavy HIPAA/privacy regulations
- Medical law compliance
- Solution liability too high

**❌ Legal/Government**
- Law firms with 10+ lawyers
- Government agencies
- Complex compliance needs
- Procurement too slow

**❌ Pharmaceutical/Chemical**
- Manufacturing drugs or chemicals
- Heavy regulatory oversight
- Complex traceability requirements
- Not SME-friendly solution

**❌ Insurance/Banking**
- Insurance brokers
- Money lenders
- Compliance-heavy industries
- Regulatory burden too high

**❌ Gambling/Adult**
- Casinos, betting
- Adult services
- High-risk industries
- Mission misalignment

---

### By Status/Activity

**❌ Closed/Defunct Businesses**
- "Out of business"
- "Cerrado permanentemente"
- No activity for 6+ months
- Wasted outreach effort

**Check by:**
- Recent Google Maps update (says "Temporarily closed")
- No posts on Facebook/Instagram in 6+ months
- Phone number disconnected
- Address no longer valid

**❌ Spam/Fake Accounts**
- Obvious spam business pages
- No business legitimacy signals
- Multiple identical businesses (resellers)
- Fake reviews (suspicious 5-stars only)

**Check by:**
- Google reviews contain warnings about scam
- Business changes address frequently
- Rapid ownership changes
- Overwhelmingly negative recent reviews

---

### By Location (Phase 1)

**❌ Outside Chile (Phase 1)**
- Phase 1 focus: Chile only
- Expansion: Phase 2 = regional Chile expansion, Phase 3 = LATAM
- No exceptions for Phase 1 (keep focused)

**❌ Rural/Remote Areas (Phase 1)**
- Service delivery difficult for Phase 1

### By Digital Capability

**Note:** Don't hard-exclude these—just score lower (40-50). Some traditional businesses are responsive.

## How to Check If Business Fits

### Verification Workflow

**Step 1: Google Maps Search**
```
Find business → Check:
├─ Employee count visible? (photos, reviews mention "team")
├─ Address valid? (street address, not PO box)
├─ Phone works? (try to call)
├─ Recent reviews? (activity signal)
└─ Industry clear? (matches target list)
```

**Step 2: Website Check (if exists)**
```
Visit website → Look for:
├─ "About" page → Employee count, location, founding date
├─ "Team" page → Staff count verification
├─ "Contact" page → Phone, email, address
├─ Legitimacy → No red flags, professional design
└─ Activity → Recent blog posts or updates
```

**Step 3: Social Media Check**
```
Facebook/Instagram → Assess:
├─ Page legitimate? (business info filled out)
├─ Recent activity? (posts within 30 days)
├─ Engagement? (comments, likes on posts)
├─ Follower count? (real followers or bots?)
└─ Contact info? (email, phone, WhatsApp visible)
```

**Step 4: Chamber/Registry Check (Optional)**
```
Mercado Público or Chamber site → Verify:
├─ Business registered? (official status)
├─ RUT valid? (tax ID exists)
├─ Contact info matches? (same address/phone)
└─ Member status? (legitimacy signal)
```

## Industry-Specific Notes

### High Propensity to Buy

These industries show highest adoption rates for SME solutions:

1. **Barbershops/Salons** - High pain point (scheduling), Instagram-native, quick decision
2. **Cafés** - Young owners (tech-friendly), high volume, clear ROI
3. **Professional Services** - High invoice values, clear business case
4. **Online E-commerce** - Already digital, hungry for tools

### Medium Propensity to Buy

These need more warm-up but valuable once converted:

1. **Mini-markets** - Price-sensitive but large market size
2. **Cleaning Services** - Value ROI heavily, but may resist tech
3. **Fitness** - Young demographic, social media presence
4. **Tutoring** - High margin, but owner = educator (not natural tech adopter)

### Lower Propensity to Buy

These require stronger value props or are lower priority:

1. **Handyman/Maintenance** - Often solo, phone-only, older demographic
2. **Bakeries** - Family businesses, traditional, resistant to change
3. **Small restaurants** - Thin margins, cash-focused, high churn
4. **Delivery services** - Already use WhatsApp, may lack infrastructure for billing

## FAQ: Industry Filtering

**Q: "I found a law firm with 8 people. Include?"**
A: Yes—8 people = below 10 threshold. Law firms have real pain (client management, billing). Include, but may need custom angle.

**Q: "E-commerce store with 3 people = include?"**
A: Absolutely. E-commerce shops are high-propensity (already digital, need fulfillment help).

**Q: "Found a beautician (solo) with no website. Include?"**
A: Yes—score may be 50-60 (low digital presence), but still worth contacting. Some solo operators are responsive.

**Q: "Bank branch with 6 employees?"**
A: No—exclude. Financial institution = regulated, compliance-heavy, won't adopt external solutions.

**Q: "Dental clinic (2 dentists + 1 receptionist)?"**
A: Caution—dental = regulated healthcare. Unless you have healthcare compliance solution, exclude.

**Q: "Uber driver (solo, uses Uber app)?"**
A: No—not a micro-business owner in target sense. Already using platform, not decision-maker.
